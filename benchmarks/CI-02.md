# CI-02 ranking benchmark

Twenty real notice pages were checked. The [frozen cohort](ci02-sample.csv) includes its UTC cutoff, exact source URLs and row fingerprints. Within four strata, records were sorted by reference and five evenly spaced positions were selected. The strata were technical-title RFPs or standing offers, near matches using an arrangement or qualification vehicle, RFIs or elapsed deadlines, and unrelated goods or construction controls. Sampling used notice metadata and title phrases without reading the application's fit or score. The sample is deliberately stratified and cannot estimate rates across all tenders.

[Expected labels](ci02-labels.psv) were committed before comparing the shortlist. The linked official notice's description, bid route, deadline and scope were the documentary basis. A restriction is recorded when stated, even when the bidder's compliance cannot be verified. No positive solo-eligibility label was inferred from a notice or from missing bidder evidence. No permitted partner route was established in the sample. Full annex requirements and bidder credentials remain unknown where supporting evidence was unavailable.

## Results

| Measure | Baseline | After fix |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Sampled notices in Recommended | 4 | 1 |
| Recommended with documented restricted route and no verified prequalification | 3 | 0 |
| Recommended with an unresolved product capability | 1 | 1 |
| Confirmed eligible solo bids | 0 | 0 |
| Eligibility abstentions for bidder credentials | 20/20 | 20/20 |
| Confirmed eligibility among qualified predictions | Undefined (no qualified predictions) | Undefined (no qualified predictions) |

If Recommended is read as an eligibility prediction, confirmed positives were 0 of 4 before and 0 of 1 afterward. The one remaining record is a partial service match requiring review. The benchmark cannot estimate recall for confirmed eligible bids.

The change holds identified restricted arrangements out of Recommended pending a complete eligibility review. The app keeps the underlying eligibility unknown while evidence is absent and keeps the notice searchable. The 20 records had no disclosed feed budget or estimated profit. The targeted regression also checked missing insurance, references and delivery cost when a ceiling and revenue were supplied. Expected hours are not a separate input. They can inform the owner's delivery cost and capacity assessment.

Replay in the original local database with `py -3 -m benchmarks.replay_ci02`. The script rejects changed sample row hashes. A newer database can have different shortlist positions or lack the original source snapshots. Restore a copy of the original database for exact comparisons. The benchmark data in Git contains source identifiers and documentary annotations without the SQLite file or local review data.

The [backup and restore procedure](../README.md#back-up-and-restore-local-research) was tested with a sample saved decision, notes and eligibility review on an open WAL database. The command refuses to replace an existing database and verifies the recovered copy.

A second check used a disposable copy of the live database. A sample federal review survived actual Open and New downloads and a successful fast-forward-only Git pull check. The branch was already up to date with the remote changes at that check.
